# ShopOS user guide (for shops)

## Getting started

1. Open the ShopOS address and choose **Create an account** (or `/signup`). Enter your shop's name,
   your name, email and a password (10+ characters).
2. Confirm your email from the message we send, then sign in. Your shop starts a **14-day free trial**.
3. Follow the checklist on the dashboard: add products, make a first sale, add a customer, invite staff,
   add the shop's phone/address for receipts, and turn on the day-end report.

ShopOS works on any computer, laptop, tablet or phone browser. On a till, use **Install app** in the
browser menu so it opens full-screen and keeps working offline.

## Daily work

- **POS** — tap or search products (barcode scanners type into the search box and press Enter). Choose
  Cash, Card or Credit (khata). For credit, pick the customer; anything not paid now goes on their
  khata. **F2** search, **F9** complete the sale, **Esc** clear the search. The receipt appears after
  the sale with an invoice number; **Print receipt** prints only the receipt.
- **Sales** — find any sale by invoice number, customer or product; reprint it (marked DUPLICATE COPY);
  managers and owners can **Return items** (money comes off the customer's khata first, the rest in
  cash, or back to the card).
- **Customers** — who owes what; **Receive payment** records part or full payment; **WhatsApp
  reminder** opens WhatsApp with a polite message; **Ledger** shows every sale and payment.
- **Inventory** — products with photo, SKU/barcode, cost and sale price, stock and reorder level;
  **Adjust stock** with a reason (damage, count correction); low stock is flagged.
- **Suppliers & Purchases** — record stock you buy (updates stock and, optionally, cost prices);
  anything unpaid goes on what you owe the supplier; **Pay supplier** when you pay.
- **Expenses** — rent, bills, wages, chai… so profit is real profit.
- **Reports** — sales, profit, best sellers, payment methods, by any date range; export CSV.
- **Day end** — the day's sales, profit, expenses, khata and cash expected in the drawer;
  **Share on WhatsApp** or **Send now**; in Settings choose a time to receive it every day by email
  (and WhatsApp, when the service has it switched on).

## Offline

If the internet drops, the POS keeps selling; sales are saved on the device and sent when the
connection is back (Settings → Sync log shows them). Don't clear the browser's data while sales are
waiting to sync.

## Staff

Owner → **Staff** → invite by email and role. The link is valid for 7 days. **Disable** signs a person
out everywhere immediately; change their role at any time.

## Billing

**Billing** shows your trial or paid period, how to pay, and your payment history. Pay by the methods
shown, then **send the payment screenshot** on the same page (or WhatsApp). When a trial or paid period
ends the shop becomes read-only (you can still see everything and pay) until the payment is recorded.

## Account

**Account**: change your password (signs out your other devices). Forgot it? **Forgot password** on
the sign-in page emails a reset link valid for one hour.
